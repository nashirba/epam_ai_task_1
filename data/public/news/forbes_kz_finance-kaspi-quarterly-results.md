# Kaspi.kz reports 22% revenue growth in Q1 2026

Kaspi.kz (KSPI on Nasdaq) released its Q1 2026 results, reporting consolidated revenue of 412 billion tenge, up 22% year on year, and net income of 178 billion tenge, up 17%. Adjusted EBITDA margin held at 50.8%, slightly down from 52.1% a year earlier on increased investments in international expansion.

Segment performance:
- Marketplace platform: GMV 2.1 trillion tenge (+27% YoY), take rate 8.4%, active merchants up 18% YoY.
- Payments: total payment volume 6.8 trillion tenge (+19% YoY), 13.9 million active consumers monthly.
- Fintech: net loan portfolio 4.2 trillion tenge (+12% YoY), cost-of-risk 1.6%, NIM 8.4%.

The company's recent acquisition in Turkey (Hepsiburada) is now consolidating; integration is on track and accretive impact is expected from Q3 2026. International revenue contribution rose to 14% of total in Q1 from 8% a year earlier.

Kaspi management reiterated its full-year 2026 guidance of 18–20% revenue growth and 14–16% net income growth, alongside a maintenance of the 50%-of-net-income quarterly dividend policy. The Q1 dividend declared was 850 tenge per common share.

"Kaspi continues to be a unique compounder in EM tech-fintech. The marketplace flywheel remains strong locally and the international optionality is cleaner than peers'. The main risk is execution risk in Turkey," wrote an analyst at JPMorgan in a follow-up note. KSPI shares on Nasdaq closed at $128.40, +3.1% on the day.
