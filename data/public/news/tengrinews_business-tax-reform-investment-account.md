# Individual investment accounts to launch in Kazakhstan in 2027

The government of Kazakhstan announced on April 15 that the long-discussed Individual Investment Account (IIA) framework will be enacted by January 2027. The IIA will allow KZ-resident individuals to invest in KASE-listed equities, bonds, and qualifying foreign instruments through a tax-advantaged account.

Two account variants will be available, modelled loosely on the Russian and US Roth IRA frameworks:
- Type A — annual contribution-based tax deduction up to 1.5 million tenge per year.
- Type B — full exemption from individual income tax on capital gains, dividends and interest from instruments held in the account, subject to a five-year minimum holding period.

The total annual contribution limit will be 5 million tenge, with no lifetime cap. Account holders may switch between Types A and B once during the account's life, but cannot have both types simultaneously.

Industry response has been positive. KASE estimates that the new framework could attract 200–300 thousand new retail accounts in the first two years and add 200–400 billion tenge to annual on-exchange volumes. Brokers Halyk Finance, Jusan Invest and Freedom Finance are already preparing UI updates and educational materials.

A pilot programme will run from October 2026 with five licensed brokers participating; the public launch is scheduled for January 2027. Treasury implementation guidance and the official tax-code amendments are expected to be published in early Q3 2026.

"This is the most important capital-markets development in Kazakhstan since the introduction of the unified pension system. It will fundamentally improve household-savings allocation efficiency over the long run," commented an AIFC managing director.
