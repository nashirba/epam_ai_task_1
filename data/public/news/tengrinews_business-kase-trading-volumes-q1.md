# KASE quarterly trading volumes hit a record high

KASE reported total Q1 2026 trading volume of 192.4 trillion tenge across all instruments, a 19% increase year on year and a record for any quarter. Equity trading volume was 612 billion tenge (+34% YoY), bond trading volume was 14.8 trillion tenge (+22% YoY), and FX market volume was 156 trillion tenge (+15% YoY).

Average daily trading volume on the equity market reached 9.7 billion tenge in Q1, lifted by strong investor interest in HSBK, KAP, and KCEL. Retail-investor share of equity volume rose to 32% from 27% a year earlier, reflecting growth in mobile-app-based brokerage offerings from Kaspi, Halyk, and Freedom.

The number of active brokerage accounts in Kazakhstan crossed 1.7 million at end-Q1, up from 1.45 million at end-2025. Of these, approximately 1.1 million accounts had at least one transaction in the past 90 days. The KASE Index gained 9.6% in Q1 and a further 2.6% month-to-date in April.

KASE management announced plans to launch new corporate-bond ETF instruments in Q3 2026 and expand market-making programmes for top-10 KASE Index constituents. A pilot of a fully retail-accessible single-stock futures contract on HSBK is also under regulatory review.

International index providers MSCI and FTSE confirmed in April that Kazakhstan would remain on the watch-list for upgrade from Frontier to Emerging Market status. Regulatory transparency, free-float depth, and the new T+2 settlement cycle are key remaining items.
