# Mortgage rates in Kazakhstan: a comparison across the major banks

Standard market mortgage rates in Kazakhstan are at multi-year highs as a result of the elevated NBK base rate. As of April 22, 2026, the most competitive non-subsidised mortgage offers from major banks were:

- Halyk Bank: 19.0% APR for primary-market property, 20.5% for secondary, max LTV 80%, max term 25 years.
- Kaspi Bank: 19.5% APR for both primary and secondary, max LTV 75%, term up to 20 years.
- Bank CenterCredit: 18.8% APR for primary, 20.0% for secondary, max LTV 70%, term up to 25 years.
- Jusan Bank: 19.2% APR for primary, 20.8% for secondary, max LTV 70%, term up to 20 years.
- ForteBank: 19.5% APR for primary, 21.0% for secondary, max LTV 75%, term up to 25 years.

By contrast, the state-subsidised "7-20-25" programme provides 7% APR (5% for families with two or more children after the recent expansion). The subsidised programme is administered through Otbasy Bank (formerly Zhilstroysberbank) and has eligibility rules including a maximum apartment price.

For a typical buyer purchasing a 36-million-tenge apartment in Almaty with a 20% down payment, the difference between the market rate and the subsidised rate over a 20-year term is approximately 25–28 million tenge in total interest. This explains the dominance of subsidised programmes in primary-market sales — over 78% of new-home transactions in Q1 2026 used the "7-20-25" or related schemes.

Analysts expect market mortgage rates to fall by 100–200 bps over the rest of 2026 if the NBK base-rate easing cycle continues.
