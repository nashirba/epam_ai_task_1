# Astana International Financial Centre attracted record FDI in Q1

Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC) reported $1.42 billion in registered investments by participating companies in Q1 2026, the strongest quarter in the centre's history. The number of registered AIFC-resident companies passed 3,200, with 184 new registrations during the quarter.

Tech and fintech accounted for the largest share of new entrants. Notable Q1 highlights include the launch of a regional headquarters by a Singapore-based payments firm, the opening of a Central Asia office by a major UK insurance broker, and a $90 million digital-asset infrastructure deal under AIFC's special regulatory regime.

The AIFC court reported 47 new commercial cases filed in Q1 — up 30% year on year — across investment, banking and IP disputes. The centre's English-law framework and independent judiciary continue to be cited by international investors as differentiating factors versus other Central Asia jurisdictions.

AIX, the AIFC's stock exchange, completed its first cross-listing of a Saudi Arabian fund in March, expanding the GCC-Central Asia investment corridor. Total AIX equity-market capitalisation reached $32 billion, with average daily trading volume up 22% year on year.

"AIFC has matured into the dominant regional capital-markets and corporate hub for Central Asia and the Caspian region. The combination of regulatory certainty, English-language documentation, and tax incentives has been a meaningful pull factor — particularly for firms exiting other regional jurisdictions," noted a senior financial-services partner at one of the Big Four advisories with a Nur-Sultan office.
