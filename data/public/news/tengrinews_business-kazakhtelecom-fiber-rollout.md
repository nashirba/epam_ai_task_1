# Kazakhtelecom accelerates GPON fiber rollout to mid-sized cities

Kazakhtelecom (KZTK) confirmed plans to extend its GPON optical fiber footprint to all cities with population above 50,000 by the end of 2027. The 65-billion-tenge programme will see new connections in Atyrau, Petropavlovsk, Kostanay, Pavlodar, Semey and a number of district centres.

The company expects fixed-broadband ARPU to expand from current 4,200 KZT to 4,800–5,000 KZT over the rollout horizon, driven by the migration of legacy ADSL customers to GPON tariffs. Average household download speeds will move from the current 50 Mbps median to 200+ Mbps.

Q1 2026 results: revenue of 195 billion tenge (+5.4% YoY), EBITDA of 92 billion tenge (+6.8% YoY), EBITDA margin 47%. The acquisition of a controlling stake in mobile operator Tele2/Altel is now in the consolidation phase; integration synergies are guided at 18–22 billion tenge annually starting 2027.

Kazakhtelecom shares (KZTK) on KASE traded at 36,800 tenge as of May 2, broadly unchanged year-to-date. The company has a stable 50%-of-net-income dividend policy, implying current yield of approximately 5.6%.

"Fiber is a structural growth story for Kazakhtelecom over the next three to five years. The challenge is execution discipline — keeping capex within plan while managing labour shortages in regional cities," noted an analyst at Halyk Finance. He retained a 'buy' recommendation with a 12-month target price of 41,500 tenge.
