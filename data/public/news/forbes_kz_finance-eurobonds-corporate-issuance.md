# Kazakhstan corporate eurobond pipeline ramps up in Q2 2026

Following the sovereign Republic of Kazakhstan placement on April 9, the corporate eurobond pipeline for Q2 2026 has expanded materially. According to bank syndicate desks, the following issuers are in active preparation:

- Halyk Bank: USD-denominated senior unsecured notes, expected size $400–500 million, tenor 5 years.
- KazMunayGas: USD-denominated benchmark notes, expected size $1.0–1.5 billion, dual-tranche 7Y/10Y.
- Kazatomprom: USD-denominated debut bond, expected size $300–500 million, tenor 5–7 years.
- KEGOC: KZT-denominated 10-year domestic notes via KASE, with EBRD anchor investor.

Investor sentiment toward Kazakhstan credit has improved meaningfully through April. The 5-year USD CDS spread for the sovereign tightened from 110 bps at end-March to 92 bps as of April 28, the tightest since 2022. Domestic-currency bond yields have also compressed by 30–50 bps across the curve.

"The combination of a stable macro picture, the sovereign rating affirmation by Fitch, and growing global EM-IG demand supports the new-issue window," said a Halyk Finance fixed-income strategist. He added that the most attractive issues for Kazakhstan-resident investors with USD savings are likely to be Halyk Bank and Kazatomprom, given their familiar credit and likely premium to sovereign of 80–120 bps at issue.

Domestic banks have also indicated they will likely issue tenge-denominated subordinated capital instruments later in 2026 to top up Tier 2 capital ratios as the central bank phases in updated capital rules under Basel-IV-aligned framework.
