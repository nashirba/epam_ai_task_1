# NBK history — provenance

| File | Source URL | Snapshot date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| base_rate_history.json | https://www.nationalbank.kz/en/baserate | 2026-05-02 | Last 24 months of NBK base rate decisions, manually transcribed from public press releases. |
| fx_history.json | https://www.nationalbank.kz/en/exchangerates/officialrates | 2026-05-02 | Weekly samples of KZT/USD, KZT/EUR, KZT/RUB official rates from 2025-11-02 through 2026-05-02. |
| gold_history.json | https://www.nationalbank.kz/en/news/altyngold | 2026-05-02 | Monthly NBK gold fixings (KZT per gram) for the trailing 12 months. |

NBK exchange rates, base-rate decisions, and gold fixings are factual public data and not subject to copyright. Source URLs are recorded for reproducibility. Where multiple daily quotes exist within a week the first weekday closing fix was retained.
